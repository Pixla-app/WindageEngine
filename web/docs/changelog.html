Changelog

1.0.0 — 2026-10-14

Initial release. A Swift 6 and Metal 4 game engine for the Mac, extracted from working games.

Added

  • Frame pacing: frames in flight, a fixed simulation step, and a clock that keeps time with the window hidden. GameLoop, FrameRenderer, FixedTimestep.
  • Rendering: reversed-Z depth, an HDR scene with tone mapping and exposure in stops, physically based materials (base color, normal, occlusion–roughness–metallic and emission maps), and image-based lighting from the sky.
  • Light and shadow: cascaded sun shadows, and spot and point lights binned into clusters, each able to cast. ShadowMap, ClusteredLights.
  • Meshes: loading through Model I/O, with USD's orientation and units handled. MeshLoader.
  • Many copies: instanced drawing, one draw per kind of prop. InstanceBatches, InstanceBuffer.
  • Detail by distance: simpler tiers of every mesh made when it loads, and the tier a copy's distance earns. MeshDetail, using meshoptimizer's simplifier (MIT).
  • Culling: frustum culling for the screen and for each shadow cascade. Frustum.
  • Characters: skinned animation from USD, cross-fades, and things hung off joints. SkeletonPose, TransformHierarchy.
  • A body with feet: stairs, slopes, lifts, turntables and being pushed, with the same result at any step rate. CharacterController.
  • Particles: sparks, embers and dust, simulated on the fixed step and drawn in one instanced draw. ParticleSystem.
  • Levels: a versioned level format, and an editor you use inside your running game. Level, LevelEditor.
  • Sound: positional audio and looping ambience that belongs to a place. AudioEngine.
  • Input and settings: keys and controllers a player rebinds, per-device sensitivity, dead zones and invert, and settings that save. InputBindings, SaveStore.
  • The HUD: text and shapes drawn after the tone map. HUDRenderer.
  • Your game's name: a splash screen and the engine's mark. SplashScreen, EngineMark.
  • Checks: scripted walks and recorded frames, so a change that moves the player or the picture fails a check instead of going unnoticed. Tools/walks.py, Tools/frames.py.
  • A sample game built only against the public API. swift run WindageSample.
